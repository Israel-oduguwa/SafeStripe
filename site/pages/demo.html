What actually happened

The failure is real.
The motion explains it.

The evidence was recorded on 7 October 2026 using the published safestripe@0.3.0 package, Express and Cloud Firestore.

01

A declined card stays unpaid.

Stripe rejected the insufficient-funds test card. SafeStripe's saved order had no paid receipt. The customer then supplied a working test card in the same Checkout Session.

02

The signed result reaches storage.

The successful retry produced an authenticated Firestore receipt for the expected order, checkout and USD amount. SafeStripe does not turn a failed card into a successful one by itself.

03

Replays preserve the original receipt.

A separate test requested ten genuine Stripe event replays. Ten additional duplicate admissions were recorded; that payment's original receipt and paid time stayed unchanged.
